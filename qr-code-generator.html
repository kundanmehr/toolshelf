Type or paste a link, text or contact details and the QR code appears. Download it as an SVG, which stays sharp at any print size.

Common questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. The text is encoded directly into the code, with no tracking link, so it works as long as you keep the image.

Why download as SVG?

SVG is a vector format, so you can scale it to a business card or a poster without losing quality.
