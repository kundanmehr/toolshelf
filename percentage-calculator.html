Find a percentage of a number, or what percent one number is of another.

How to use the percentage calculator

Use the first pair of boxes for questions like 'what is 15% of 200', and the second pair for 'what percent of 80 is 20'.

Common questions

How do I calculate X% of Y?

Multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30.

How do I find what percent A is of B?

Divide A by B and multiply by 100. For example, 20 out of 80 is 20 ÷ 80 × 100 = 25%.
