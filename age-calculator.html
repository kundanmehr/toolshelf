Age Calculator

Find an exact age and the days until the next birthday.

How to use the age calculator

Enter a date of birth to see the age in years, months and days, the total days lived, and the countdown to the next birthday.

Common questions

How is age calculated?

It counts full years, then months and days from the birth date to today's date on your device.

Is the date stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is saved.
