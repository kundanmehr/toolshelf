Text Compare

Paste two versions of a text to see which lines were added or removed.

How to use the text compare

Paste the original on the left and the changed version on the right, then press Compare. Added lines are green and removed lines are red.

Common questions

How does the comparison work?

It compares line by line and finds the longest sequence of lines the two texts share, then marks the rest as added or removed.

Is there a size limit?

It works best up to about 500 lines per side, so the page stays fast.
