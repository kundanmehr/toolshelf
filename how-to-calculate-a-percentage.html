How to Calculate a Percentage: 5 Easy Methods

Five simple ways to work out percentages in your head or on paper, with real examples for discounts, tips and changes over time.

A percentage is just a number out of 100. Once you know a few reliable methods, you can work out discounts, tips and exam scores without reaching for your phone, although the percentage calculator on this site is there when you want a quick check.

1. Multiply by the decimal

Turn the percentage into a decimal by dividing it by 100, then multiply. For 15% of 200, use 0.15 × 200 = 30.

2. Divide by 100, then multiply

This is the same idea in a different order. For 15% of 200: 200 ÷ 100 = 2, then 2 × 15 = 30. It is handy when the numbers are round.

3. Use the 10% shortcut

Ten percent of any number is that number with the decimal point moved one place to the left. So 10% of 80 is 8, and 5% is half of that, which is 4. To find 15% of 80, add them: 8 + 4 = 12. This is the fastest way to work out a tip.

4. Find what percent one number is of another

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. If you scored 42 out of 60, then 42 ÷ 60 = 0.7, and 0.7 × 100 = 70%.

5. Work out a percentage change

Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. If a price goes from 20 to 25, the change is (25 − 20) ÷ 20 × 100 = 25% higher. If it goes from 25 down to 20, it is (20 − 25) ÷ 25 × 100 = −20%, a 20% drop. Notice that the two answers differ, because each one is measured against a different starting value.

A quick discount example

A jacket costs 80 and is 30% off. The discount is 0.30 × 80 = 24, so you pay 80 − 24 = 56.
