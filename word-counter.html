Word Counter

Count words, characters and sentences as you type, and see how long your text takes to read.

How to use the word counter

Paste or type your text and the counts update immediately. It is useful for essays, social posts, product descriptions and any text with a length limit.

Common questions

How is reading time calculated?

It divides the word count by about 220 words per minute, the average adult reading speed, and rounds to the nearest minute.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. The count happens in your browser and your text never leaves your device.
