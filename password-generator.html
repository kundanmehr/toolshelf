How to use the password generator

Choose a length and character types, then generate. Passwords are made on your device with the browser's cryptographic random generator and are never sent anywhere.

Common questions

How long should a password be?

Use at least 14 to 16 characters and a different password for every account. A password manager makes that practical.

Are these passwords truly random?

Yes. They come from the browser's crypto.getRandomValues function, which is designed for security use.
